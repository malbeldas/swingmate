Why pay for nights you spend on site?

On a 2-and-1 swing you sleep in town about 8 nights out of 21. A full-time lease charges you for all of them, and most rooms online don't care about your roster.

How it works

Looking for a place

  1. Search by suburb and add your dates or your roster.
  2. See which homes are free for the nights you need.
  3. Message the host. You only need an account when you send.

Have a room to share

  1. List your room and mark when it's free, or let your own roster fill the calendar.
  2. Verify your email and mobile, then publish.
  3. Hear from people whose roster fits your home.

Built for trust

  • Your exact address is never shown. Listings show an approximate area only.
  • Your email, mobile and employer stay private.
  • Report or block anyone, any time.

Questions

When does SwingMate open?

We're starting with a pilot in Perth. Follow us on Instagram to hear the day it opens.

How much does it cost?

Nothing during the pilot, for hosts and for people looking for a place.

Do I pay the rent through SwingMate?

No. SwingMate helps you find each other and talk. You agree the rent and how to pay it directly with the host.

I don't have a fixed roster. Can I still use it?

Yes. Search with your dates instead, and every home shows if it's free for them.

Who can list a room?

Anyone with a room or a home to share in Perth. Hosts verify their email and mobile before their listing goes live.

Have a room in Perth?

Be one of the first hosts in the pilot. Send us a message on Instagram and we'll help you set up your listing when we open.
