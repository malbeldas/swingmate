How it works

Looking for a place

  1. Search by suburb and add your dates or your roster.
  2. See which homes are free for the nights you need.
  3. Message the host. You only need an account when you send.

Have a room to share

  1. List your room and mark when it's free, or let your own roster fill the calendar.
  2. Verify your email and mobile, then publish.
  3. Hear from people whose roster fits your home.

Built for trust

  • Your exact address is never shown. Listings show an approximate area only.
  • Your email, mobile and employer stay private.
  • Report or block anyone, any time.
