SwingMate

How it works

SwingMate helps FIFO, DIDO and BIBO workers share a home around their rosters. You find a place that is free the nights you're in town, or offer your room for the nights you're on site.

Looking for a place

  1. Search by suburb and add your dates or your roster.
  2. See which homes are free for the nights you need.
  3. Message the host. You only need an account when you send.
Search homes

Have a room to share

  1. List your room and mark when it's free, or let your own roster fill the calendar.
  2. Verify your email and mobile, then publish.
  3. Hear from people whose roster fits your home.
List a place

You agree the details between you

SwingMate doesn't take payments, bookings or contracts. You talk in the chat, see the place, and agree the rent, the bond and the dates directly with the other person.
