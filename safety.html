Safety

Most people on SwingMate are workers looking for the same thing you are. These are the checks that keep it that way.

What stays private

  • Your exact address is never shown. Listings show an approximate area only.
  • Your email, mobile and employer stay private.
  • Hosts verify their mobile before they publish.

If something feels wrong

  • SwingMate warns you when a message asks for money before you've seen the place.
  • Report or block anyone, any time, from the listing or the conversation. We review reports within 24 hours.
  • Never pay a deposit to hold a place you haven't seen.
